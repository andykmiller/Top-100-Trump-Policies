Project Log

Development Summary

The project began as a request for a public-facing list of the top 100 Trump accomplishments or impacts, including positive and negative consequences without judging them as good or bad.

The public title was changed to "The 100 Most Consequential Trump Policies and Actions (2017-2026)" to avoid implying praise or criticism.

The methodology evolved from a people-and-money-heavy formula to a more balanced formula that includes institutional permanence, duration, international impact, and historical significance.

The editorial process changed after early drafts became too generic. The current standard requires specific descriptions, concrete facts, supportive and critical analysis, comparative ranking logic, and duplicate-text checks.
