FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Trump's most consequential policy?

Under this methodology, the highest-ranked actions are those combining broad reach, large economic or institutional magnitude, and long duration. The top entry may change if future evidence changes the scoring.

Why not call these accomplishments?

Accomplishment implies approval. This site ranks consequence, including actions that supporters praise, critics oppose, and historians may judge differently over time.

Are the rankings political?

The topic is political, but the method is designed to be transparent rather than partisan. Each entry includes supportive and critical interpretations.

Can rankings change?

Yes. The Dynamic Ranking Rule allows changes when new actions occur, litigation changes implementation, new evidence emerges, or entries should be merged or split.

Why include both supportive and critical analysis?

Many readers will not click every source. Short summaries of the main arguments help readers understand the debate before they follow links.
