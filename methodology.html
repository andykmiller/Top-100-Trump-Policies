Methodology

Project goal

This project is to work with ChatGPT to create a non-partisan assessment of President Trump. My hope is for people on the right and on the left find some common ground, some common facts they can agree upon. I feel the country is too polarized right now. People may disagree with ranking and order, but I've told the AI to be neutral about it, try to avoid all the emotion associated with politics. Also, I acknowledge that AI may have some biases, but my sense is it's treated these topics fairly and evenly.

Original ChatGPT prompt:

Please make a web page that is a list of the top 100 Trump accomplishments or impacts, both good and bad without judgement as to good or bad, ranked in descending order of impact. By impact, I'm, not sure, but maybe combination of money and number of people impacted? Examples would include deportation (legal vs. illegal vs. criminal/illegal), spending cuts or increases to science, art, health, welfare, etc. Tax changes, environment, climate change, deficit changes. Try to get a factual, non-partisan assessment. Add links for each item to both a conservative and liberal interpretation of the impact.

So far, I made 116 additional prompts and answers to create the current website. I may publish some more or all of the transcript of the conversation in the future.

All the content on this site is generated by ChatGPT, other than the above.

How the Ranking Works

This project ranks consequences, not virtue. A policy can be ranked highly because it changed many lives, moved large amounts of money, altered institutions, or shaped history, even if readers strongly disagree about whether the change was good.

Purpose and Intent

The site is intended for the U.S. general public: readers who want a clearer answer to what the Trump administrations actually did and why those actions mattered. The goal is not to persuade readers toward a partisan conclusion. The goal is to make the major actions legible, comparable, and open to scrutiny.

Each entry tries to answer four practical questions: what changed, who was affected, why supporters thought it mattered, and why critics thought it mattered. The ranking is an editorial judgment, but it is made transparent through factor scores, source links, and a public change log.

The site deliberately uses the phrase "consequential" rather than "accomplishment" or "failure." Some actions are celebrated by supporters and condemned by critics. Others are mixed, partially reversed, or still unfolding. The ranking is about scale and durability of consequence.

Nonpartisan Treatment

Entries include both supportive and critical analyses because impact is often contested. Supportive analysis is not endorsement. Critical analysis is not condemnation. Each section should present the strongest version of the argument readers are likely to encounter from serious supporters or critics.

Where a policy is still developing, the site gives more weight to recent sources. Older sources remain useful for original implementation, legal texts, agency rules, and primary records.

Dynamic Ranking Rule

The ranking is not frozen forever. Under RM-012, major new developments can cause entries to move, merge, split, or be removed. The Iran escalation entry is an example of why the project needs dynamic review: consequential events can emerge after an earlier list seemed complete.

Editorial Quality Standard

Production entries are expected to pass the Magazine Article Test: if the title were removed, a knowledgeable reader should still know which action is being discussed. Boilerplate paragraphs are not acceptable. "Why This Rank?" sections should compare the entry with nearby higher- and lower-ranked items rather than repeating generic scoring language.

Limitations

The scores are estimates, not mathematical facts. Some effects are hard to measure, especially institutional, diplomatic, and long-term historical consequences. Readers are encouraged to review the sources, compare the factor scores, and treat the ranking as a transparent argument rather than a final verdict.
